About Élan

[ 01 ] Who We Are

Élan sits at the intersection of business, finance, and culture. We examine the market dynamics and commercial forces behind art, fashion, hospitality, and experience — industries where creativity and capital are inseparable, yet rarely examined together.

[ 02 ] What We Do

We approach these industries with the curiosity of a cultural publication and the analytical discipline of an investor. Our coverage connects what people desire with how companies operate, compete, and grow — examining pricing power, brand equity, market shifts, and the influence of consumer behavior on business strategy.

[ 03 ] Who We Write For

Our editorial voice is informed, curious, and approachable. We are writing for students, professionals, investors, and creatives who want to understand the economics behind the brands and experiences they encounter. No finance background required — only curiosity.

[ 04 ] Our Ethos

We understand luxury through the quality, care, creativity, and meaning behind a product or experience. Price and exclusivity are part of the story — but so are craftsmanship, heritage, service, and the ability to make people feel something. Our long-term vision is to reimagine how print media is consumed, exploring how technology can enhance the way readers engage with editorial and advertising alike.

[ 05 ] Join Us

We are building a team of writers, editors, and designers aligned with our ethos. We welcome contributors with different areas of expertise — from financial analysis to fashion, art, and hospitality. What matters is curiosity, sound judgment, attention to detail, and a willingness to investigate beyond the surface.
